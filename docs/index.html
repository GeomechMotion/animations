Welcome to the GeomechMotion animation gallery. This collection features a series of animations developed for our graduate courses, 'Numerical Geotechnics I and II', as well as for various conferences and educational presentations. Our goal is to provide visual and intuitive explanations of complex geotechnical concepts and numerical modeling techniques. Explore the categories below to delve into the fascinating world of geomechanics.

  • Constitutive Models - Explore a variety of animations illustrating the behavior of different constitutive models used in geotechnical engineering. These visualizations help to understand the complex stress-strain relationships of soils.
  • PLAXIS - This section contains animations of mesh generation and analysis performed with PLAXIS, a leading software for geotechnical finite element analysis. Observe how meshes are created and how they deform under different loading conditions.
  • Undergraduate - Discover a set of animations specifically created for undergraduate students. These simplified visualizations aim to explain fundamental geotechnical concepts in an accessible and engaging way.
  • FLAC (WIP) - Animations created with FLAC, another powerful tool for geotechnical modeling, are currently a work in progress. Stay tuned for future updates in this section!
